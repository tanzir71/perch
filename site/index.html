One-line install

One tag. The whole picture.

Add the tracker once. Perch handles pageviews, campaigns, outbound links, and the CTA signals that tell you whether a landing page is doing its job.

index.html
<!-- Add once, just before </body> -->
<script
  src="https://perch.YOUR-ACCOUNT.workers.dev/perch.js"
  data-project-id="my-project"
  defer
></script>

<!-- Mark the clicks that matter -->
<a href="/signup" data-perch-cta="signup-hero">Start free</a>

Privacy is the architecture

Count the visit. Forget the visitor.

Perch derives the minimum useful context at the edge, hashes and discards the raw IP, and rotates anonymous identifiers every day. There is no person-level profile to browse or sell.

  • No cookies or client-side storage at all
  • No raw IP stored or returned
  • No cross-site or cross-day tracking
  • Country-level geography only
  • GDPR/CCPA-friendly, typically no banner
Privacy-policy text you can reuse

We use Perch, a cookieless analytics tool, to understand aggregate site traffic and improve this website. Perch does not store IP addresses or create cross-site profiles. Anonymous visitor identifiers rotate daily. We collect page paths, coarse country and device information, referring domains, campaign tags, and interactions such as button clicks. Do not submit personal information through analytics event properties.

FAQ

A few things worth knowing.

Short answers here. README.md and INTEGRATION.md go deeper.

Do I need a cookie banner?

Perch uses no cookies or persistent identity, so analytics alone typically does not require a consent banner. Your site may still need one for other tools; confirm requirements for your jurisdiction.

Is Perch GDPR compliant?

Perch is designed for data minimization: no raw IP storage, no cross-site profiles, daily-rotating anonymous hashes, and country-level location only. Compliance also depends on how you deploy it and what your site collects elsewhere.

What does it cost?

Perch itself is MIT-licensed. GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Workers, Access, and D1 all offer free tiers that suit personal and modest marketing traffic; a nightly summary keeps dashboard reads small. Provider usage fees may apply beyond those limits.

Can I self-host it?

Yes. That is the default model: your Worker, your D1 database, your Access policy, and your GitHub Pages site. The Deploy to Cloudflare button creates the Worker and database for you.

How accurate is it?

Perch queries every event it receives without sampling. Cookieless daily visitors and 30-minute sessions are intentionally coarse approximations, and blockers can prevent some events unless you use a first-party endpoint.

How do I add another site?

Register a new project ID, add the same tag with that ID, and instrument its CTAs. It appears automatically inside the existing project dropdown, with an install check that confirms the first events.

Will ad blockers block it?

Some privacy extensions block *.workers.dev. Serve your Worker from a subdomain of your own site, such as a.example.com, and load /perch.js from there; most of that loss goes away.

Does it work with single-page apps?

Yes. Call perch.track("pageview") after each route change. Perch reads the current path, and closes out the previous page’s scroll and time first.
