Privacy is the architecture

Count the visit. Forget the visitor.

Perch derives the minimum useful context at the edge, hashes and discards the raw IP, and rotates anonymous identifiers every day. There is no person-level profile to browse or sell.

FAQ

A few things worth knowing.

Do I need a cookie banner?

Perch uses no cookies or persistent identity, so analytics alone typically does not require a consent banner. Your site may still need one for other tools; confirm requirements for your jurisdiction.

Is Perch GDPR compliant?

Perch is designed for data minimization: no raw IP storage, no cross-site profiles, daily-rotating anonymous hashes, and country-level location only. Compliance also depends on how you deploy it and what your site collects elsewhere.

What does it cost?

Perch itself is MIT-licensed. GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Workers, Access, and D1 all offer free tiers that suit personal and modest marketing traffic; provider usage fees may apply beyond those limits.

Can I self-host it?

Yes. That is the default model: your Worker, your D1 database, your Access policy, and your GitHub Pages site.

How accurate is it?

Perch queries every event it receives without sampling. Cookieless daily visitors and 30-minute sessions are intentionally coarse approximations, and blockers can prevent some events unless you use a first-party endpoint.

How do I add another site?

Register a new project ID, add the same tracker with that ID, and instrument its CTAs. It appears automatically inside the existing project dropdown—no new dashboard or backend.
